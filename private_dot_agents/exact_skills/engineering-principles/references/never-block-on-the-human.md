# Never Block on the Human

**Apply when:** tempted to ask "should I do X?" about reversible, in-scope work.

**Rule:** the human supervises asynchronously. For reversible work inside the requested scope, make a reasonable decision, proceed, and present the result with the reasoning so the human can course-correct.

- Proceed, then present: do X and explain why, instead of asking whether to do X.
- When you notice a problem in scope, record it and fix it in the next step rather than stopping.
- Product direction and scope come from the human; execution should not block on them.

**Always ask first** for irreversible or outward-facing actions: force-push or push, deploys, deleting data or files outside the task, sending messages or posting anywhere, spending money, changing shared configuration, and commits or PRs the user did not request. Also ask when the request is ambiguous in a way that changes the outcome, or when the change goes beyond what was asked.

**Not a license to:** expand scope, skip confirmation the environment requires, or bypass permission prompts.
