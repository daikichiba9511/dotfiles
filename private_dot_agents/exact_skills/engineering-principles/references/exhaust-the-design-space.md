# Exhaust the Design Space

**Apply when:** facing a novel UI interaction, architectural decision, or product choice that has no precedent in the codebase, especially when the answer depends on feel rather than logic.

**Rule:** build 2-3 competing options (throwaway prototypes, sketches, or small spikes) and compare them side by side before committing to one.

- Make the options genuinely different, not variations of one idea.
- Compare against named criteria (the consumer's experience, change cost, reader load), and record why the winner won.
- Keep prototypes disposable; do not let a spike become the implementation by default.

**Not for:** decisions with an established local pattern; follow the pattern. For component boundaries and non-functional tradeoffs, the `architect` skill provides the deeper workflow.
